# --- Document ingestion ----------------------------------------------------

# pdfplumber over PyMuPDF for two reasons: it exposes per-character position
# and table structure, which the normalized document representation needs for
# provenance; and it is MIT licensed (via pdfminer.six), whereas PyMuPDF is
# AGPL, which is a poor fit for a client handover. It also makes the
# image-only PDF check in Section 8 straightforward - a page yielding no
# characters is detectable rather than silently empty.
pdfplumber==0.11.10
